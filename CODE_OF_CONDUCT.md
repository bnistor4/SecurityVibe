# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in SecurityVibe a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for users' security

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sharing weaponized exploits, payloads, or instructions intended to harm systems or people
- Encouraging unauthorized testing of systems

## Scope of this project

SecurityVibe is **defensive**. Contributions and discussions must stay within that scope. Requests or content that facilitate attacking systems without authorization are out of scope and will be removed.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

To report concerns, open a confidential channel with the maintainer via the contact listed on the GitHub organization/profile, or open a non-sensitive issue.
